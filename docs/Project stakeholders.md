# Project stakeholders

| Name of Stakeholder    | Type of stakeholder | Status   | Expectations/ Interests                                                                                                                            | Conflicts                                                                                                                                                                                                   | Benefits                                                                                                                                                                                                                        | Team response                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| ---------------------- | ------------------- | -------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Us                     | Development team    | Positive | It's in our interest if our application succeeds, even if our expectations are more level headed.                                                  | The development team is comprised of students and since this is a passion project, there is no pay. There are possible time conflicts between student obligations and development obligations.              | We benefit massively in our professional careers, if our applications succeeds. It is a great addition to our project portfolio.                                                                                                | Approaching the development in an incremental approach, with a digestible workload each week and strong communication, so the team members don't feel unmotivated or overwhelmed by the obligations.                                                                                                                           |
| Gamers                 | End users           | Positive | The expectations for the application is a user-based community for reviewing, rating and tracking video games from different platforms.            | Conflicts could arise in the case that gamers prefer some other way of tracking games they've played, they play games only on one platform or they are satisfied with the current game reviewing platforms. | The more gamers use the application, the better the user experience is, since there is a larger community, meaning more reviews, discussions and information available. The benefit is the exchange of knowledge between users. | We will focus on the user experience and ensure broad functionality for our application, to make it more appealing. We will conduct user testing during development, and adjust our application based on the gamers' responses.                                                                                                |
| Game critics           | End users           | Neutral  | It's in their interest if our app succeeds because interest and interaction with game reviews and discussions will surge.                          | There might be conflicts if they are sponsored by one of our competitors, like for an example, Steam.                                                                                                       | They benefit if our application is popular and if there are more people in the community. They can close the distance between real users and professional critics by having a profile on our application.                       | We believe that the service we provide is better for game critics because our application encourages more community interaction and focuses on promoting game reviews, even if we do not sponsor them. To incentivize game critics, we will implement verified profiles for them and promote their reviews on our application. |
| IGDB.com               | API provider        | Positive | The API providers, IGDB.com, want attribution for the use of their API for commercial use.                                                         | There are no major conflicts.                                                                                                                                                                               | IGDB.com get free exposure to their website and API.                                                                                                                                                                            | We will add clear attribution and we will link IGDB.com in the About Us page.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| Steam                  | Competitors         | Negative | The success of our application is not in Steam's interest, since the use and function of our application is broader for the task of game tracking. | The user community on Steam is threatened by our application, since we provide a larger framework for user interaction.                                                                                     | More reviews from our website leads to more Steam game sales.                                                                                                                                                                   | Steams library can get cluttered over time and it becomes very tedious to navigate. We will implement a more smooth and efficient library tracking experience, with a bigger assortment.                                                                                                                                       |
| Data regulatory bodies | Government          | Neutral  | Follow the guidelines and laws about user data as stated by the government.                                                                        | If we don't follow the law, we would be given a fine.                                                                                                                                                       | No benefit for the data regulatory bodies, however, users may feel more secure and trusting of our application if we ensure data protection.                                                                                    | We will follow the rules and guidelines of user data protection, as well as ensure secure authentication and database interaction.                                                                                                                                                                                             |
