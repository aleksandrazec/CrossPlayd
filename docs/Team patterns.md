# Team patterns

In the interest of bettering team dynamics and bettering morale, the following is an analysis on our team's positive and negative patterns. 
## Team's negative patterns
### Pattern 1: Ignoring low performers

A pattern that was prominent at the start of development of the application was ignoring low performers. Not everyone in the team was equally as familiar with the technology being used, which resulted in team members having variable efficiency for the tasks assigned to them. This meant that deadlines for certain tasks weren't being met and the development had unrealistic timelines set.

To battle this, the team is meeting in real life at least once a week to work and develop together. While each member still has individual tasks assigned to them, such an approach means that the team members are able to discuss issues that might arise and assist each other in difficult areas. We also adjust the deadlines and goals appropriately for each team member, so everyone is working on their tasks in a realistic and timely manner.
### Pattern 2: Being everyone's friend

Considering the fact that the team members are classmates and friends, there is a pattern of trying to avoid any sort of conflict to make sure that the private relationships are not affected by the project's development and success. However, this means that when some parts of the product weren't developed with the proper care, the members are afraid to critique the work, discuss the issues explicitly and confront each other about it.

To tackle this issue, we make sure that critiques pointed at each other's work are as objective as possible, and aren't framed as personal attacks towards the members. Also, we make sure to draw a clear line between the dynamics of members during development and outside of the work, so that the issues don't transfer between the areas, and impact our personal relationship, nor compromise the team dynamics.

### Pattern 3: Technical Perfectionist

As ambitious and motivated individuals, we sometimes find it hard to not be perfectionists during production. Balancing quality, deadlines and our personal satisfaction with the product is a complex task that requires a lot of focus and clarity during development, when it's easy to get lost in the tasks of the job. 

To assure the team is moving forward with the tasks at hand and the deadlines are still being met, we will try to define the tasks in a concrete and grounded manner. This means that the goals set should be specific, incremental and measurable, and there should be really explicit expectations for each upcoming milestone. This way, the members are sure that they aren't overachieving or spending too much time on perfecting their work.
## Team's positive patterns

### Pattern 1: Be a Teacher and a Mentor

A positive thing that the team members implement during work, is helping each other in a proper manner. When a team member is confused or stuck on some part of their task, to help them, another member comes and guides them in the right direction, providing key information or resources needed for the solution. In this situation, the members don't try to overexplain things or ramble for too long, and instead give the one asking for help space to figure it out themselves, and let them know they are available if additional clarification or help is needed. 

### Pattern 2: Be a Catalyst

In an effort to work efficiently and in agreement, team members often engage in productive conversations and discussions. When a team member seems to take lead for a particular part of the product, the others don't mind following them and discussing directly with them for the purpose of reaching common goal and consensus. Depending on the functionality/part of the product, different members are assumed for this role, depending on their expertise and passion for the task at hand.

### Pattern 3: Track Happiness

To keep morale up, the team members try to maintain an upbeat and low-stress environment during work. During work, the team members keep each others needs in mind and lift each other up. Meetings and productive conversations are held with consideration of the members temperament, mood and their current needs. Being happy and content while working means being more productive and providing higher quality of solutions, so we don't neglect this even in higher stake situations.

### Conclusion

Even though the team has some anti-patterns that might be compromising the efficiency of production, the morale is kept high and we try to battle the issues present at the moment, and are constantly improving. Considering the team is relatively new and inexperienced, as well as the fact the team dynamics are still being concretely defined and established, we find that things are moving along at a nice and healthy pace.
